# SpeakMate production image prompts

All assets were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool and saved as optimized 768 × 480 WebP files under `public/scenes/`.

## Shared art direction

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: wide mobile app scene banner for SpeakMate
Style/medium: cinematic editorial lifestyle photography, realistic and mature
Composition/framing: wide landscape with adult speakers in the left and center thirds and usable negative space near the upper right for interface overlays
Lighting/mood: natural practical light, calm, focused and welcoming
Color palette: Atlantic navy, warm paper neutrals and one restrained coral accent
Constraints: no readable text, logos, watermark, phone UI, illustration or children
```

## Asset subjects

| File | Primary request |
| --- | --- |
| `hotel.webp` | Adult East Asian traveler checking in with a professional host in a boutique hotel lobby at evening. |
| `travel.webp` | Adult East Asian traveler speaking with airline staff at a contemporary airport check-in counter. |
| `dining.webp` | Adult learner ordering from a friendly barista in a modern neighborhood café. |
| `daily.webp` | Adult customer explaining a smartphone problem to a repair technician. |
| `work.webp` | Diverse adult team listening to an East Asian colleague give a concise progress update. |
| `social.webp` | Two adults having relaxed first-time small talk at a cultural event. |
| `study.webp` | Adult international student asking a professor a thoughtful question after class. |
| `emergency.webp` | Adult traveler describing a non-emergency concern to a pharmacist. |

The app-icon prompts use the same Atlantic, coral and warm-white palette. The primary icon is a coral speech bubble with five voice bars; the maskable master adds an opaque, full-bleed Atlantic background and keeps the mark within the central safe zone.
